WEEKEND CONSUMED

One dataset, rendered twice. Two twelve-hour shifts, every hour accounted for, and seven mood scores a day across a weekend worked while studying full time.

The Infographic

A single narrative built around a radial 24-hour clock, with concentric rings for Saturday and Sunday, activity colour-coded around the dial and mood encoded alongside it. Hand-coded HTML and CSS, no libraries.

The Interactive Version

The same data, open to interrogation. Four tabs, metric and day toggles, a shift band overlay, tooltips and click-to-inspect detail. Charts drawn on HTML5 Canvas in vanilla JavaScript, no charting library.

About the data. Everything here was recorded by me. Through my final year I was in college Monday to Friday and worked two twelve-hour shifts every Saturday and Sunday. The weekend follows the same structure every week, so the timings are a pattern rather than a one-off. Mood was self-rated 1 to 10 at seven fixed transition points: waking, clocking in, first break, after eating, late shift, getting home, and bed.
